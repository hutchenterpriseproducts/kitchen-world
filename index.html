About Kitchen World

A leading supplier of premium kitchen equipment

Kitchen World is dedicated to delivering excellence to our customers. We import top-quality products from globally recognized brands and make them available in Sri Lanka at competitive prices.

Whether you are setting up a home kitchen, a restaurant, or a large-scale hotel kitchen, we provide everything you need under one roof — both retail and wholesale.

Our commitment is to ensure customer satisfaction through reliable products, excellent service, and strong after-sales support — led by proprietor M S M Zuhrie and the Kitchen World team in Colombo 12.
